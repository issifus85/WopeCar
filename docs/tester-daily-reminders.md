# WopeCar closed-test: daily tester reminders (14 days)

Send one message a day, same time each day (mid-morning works well). Replace the two placeholders:

- `[QA SHEET LINK]` - your existing QA/feedback sheet
- `[PLAY OPT-IN LINK]` - the closed-test opt-in link (only used in the welcome and the day-7 message)

Each daily message is a different task so testers open the app and use real features, which is what Google looks at. Payments: the app is connected to live payments, so every message that touches booking says to stop before paying.

Tip: start "Day 1" on the day the 14-day clock restarts (the review was Monday 5 Oct; Google counts 14 more days from then, so reapply from about Mon 19 Oct). If a tester goes quiet for two days, message them directly rather than waiting.

---

## Welcome message (send once, before Day 1)

Hi everyone, thank you for helping us test the WopeCar app on Android.

For the next 14 days we're asking you to open the app every day and try one small task. It takes 5 to 10 minutes. Each morning we'll send you that day's task.

Two quick rules:
1. Please stay opted in to the test and keep the app installed until we tell you we're done. If you drop out, it can stop us from going live.
2. Write anything that looks wrong, confusing or slow in our QA sheet: [QA SHEET LINK]. Even "this was confusing" helps.

Not installed yet? Join here: [PLAY OPT-IN LINK]

Thank you!

## Daily reminders

**Day 1 - Sign up and look around**
Good morning! Today's task: open WopeCar, create an account (or log in) and scroll through the home screen. Does everything load? Are the pictures clear? Add anything odd to the QA sheet: [QA SHEET LINK]

**Day 2 - Find a car**
Today: search for a car. Try a location (start typing and pick from the suggestions), choose dates and times, then open two or three cars and look at the photos, price and details. Anything confusing or missing? [QA SHEET LINK]

**Day 3 - Filters and favourites**
Today: use the filters (for example SUV or sedan, price) and tap the heart on a few cars. Then open your favourites. Did the filters give you what you expected? Notes here: [QA SHEET LINK]

**Day 4 - Start a booking (stop before paying)**
Today: pick a car and go through the booking steps, adding any extras you like, up to the payment screen. Please do NOT pay. Check the total looks right and the steps make sense. Tell us where you got stuck: [QA SHEET LINK]

**Day 5 - Ask a question**
Today: open a car and tap Inquiry to message the WopeCar team. Send a short question and check the chat opens and your message arrives. Any delay or error, please note it: [QA SHEET LINK]

**Day 6 - Your profile and documents**
Today: open your profile and settings. Add a profile photo, look at the documents section and try changing a setting such as dark mode or notifications. Does everything save? [QA SHEET LINK]

**Day 7 - Halfway! Check you're still in**
You're halfway through, thank you! Please check you're still opted in and the app is installed (if not, rejoin here: [PLAY OPT-IN LINK]). Today's task: also update the app from the Play Store if it shows an update, then open it. Tell us if anything changed or broke: [QA SHEET LINK]

**Day 8 - Promo code and extras**
Today: start a booking again and try adding the WopeCare protection and, if available, the driver option. Watch how the total changes. If you have a promo code, enter it. Stop before paying, as always. Any wrong price or unclear wording? [QA SHEET LINK]

**Day 9 - Save for later**
Today: build a booking and use "Save & Pay Later" instead of paying. Then go back and find it again. Was it easy to find and continue? [QA SHEET LINK]

**Day 10 - Notifications and inbox**
Today: open the Inbox and notifications. Check that old messages and alerts display properly, and that the number on the app icon (if any) makes sense. Tell us if anything looks duplicated or wrong: [QA SHEET LINK]

**Day 11 - Help and info screens**
Today: open the Help centre, FAQs, Safety Centre and the Terms and Privacy screens. Is the wording clear? Do all the links open? [QA SHEET LINK]

**Day 12 - Share your car (host side)**
Today: switch to the Share your car / vendor option and look at the application steps. You don't need to finish it. Is it clear what you'd need to do? [QA SHEET LINK]

**Day 13 - Try to break it**
Today: use the app in a rough way: switch between screens quickly, go offline for a minute then back online, rotate the phone, press back a lot. Tell us anything that crashes or freezes: [QA SHEET LINK]

**Day 14 - Final day and overall feedback**
Last day, thank you! Today: use the app any way you like for a few minutes, then add one overall note to the QA sheet: what you liked, what annoyed you, and anything you'd change. [QA SHEET LINK]
Please stay opted in until we confirm the test is complete.
